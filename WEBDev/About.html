About Rose Public School

Empowering minds and enriching lives through quality education in Mithilanchal since 1992.

OUR LEGACY

Pioneering Quality Education in Mithilanchal

Rose Public School, Darbhanga, established in 1992, is one of the oldest and largest English-medium schools in the Mithilanchal region. Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE Affiliation No. 330024), the school offers quality education from Pre-Nursery to Senior Secondary (Class XII).

Our focus is on fostering an environment where academic excellence goes hand in hand with character building, values, and holistic personality development.
