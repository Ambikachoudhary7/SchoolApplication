Academic Structure

Comprehensive curriculum designed for holistic development from Pre-Primary to Senior Secondary level.

ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

Classes Offered

Pre-Primary

Pre-Nursery, Nursery, LKG, UKG

Play-way methodology, foundational literacy, and numeracy skills.

Primary

Class 1 to Class 5

Conceptual understanding, language development, and experiential learning.

Middle School

Class 6 to Class 8

Critical thinking, project-based learning, and skill development.

Secondary

Class 9 to Class 10

Board exam preparation, career counseling, and holistic development.

Senior Secondary

Class 11 to Class 12

Specialized streams (Science, Commerce, Humanities) and competitive exam prep.

OUR APPROACH

Innovative Teaching Methodology

At Rose Public School, we believe that every child is unique and learns differently. Our teaching methodology blends traditional values with modern pedagogical techniques to create an engaging learning environment.

  • Student-centric learning approach
  • Integration of technology in classrooms
  • Experiential and project-based learning
  • Continuous and comprehensive evaluation
  • Focus on 21st-century skills (4Cs)
